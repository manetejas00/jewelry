# Product Image Generation & Quality Standards

## 1. Executive Summary
Veyora Jewels enforces studio-grade e-commerce photography standards across its fine jewellery, ethnic fashion (Chicken Curry Kurtas), and bullion coin collections. All image assets are produced, processed, and served in compliance with luxury digital retail standards.

---

## 2. Fine Jewellery Photography Standards
- **Purity & Polish**: Macro-focus on gemstone facets, pearl luster, 18K/22K yellow gold grain, and 925 sterling silver rhodium finish.
- **Lighting**: Soft diffused three-point studio lighting with high CRI (>98) to eliminate hot spots and reflections while accentuating fine filigree and kundan setting details.
- **Background**: Neutral warm ivory (`#fcfbf9`) to soft champagne gradient, matching the Veyora Maison aesthetic.
- **Angles Captured**:
  1. *Hero / Front*: Full piece straight-on view with optimal balance and proportion.
  2. *Macro Detail*: 1:1 close-up of stone setting, hallmark, clasp, or filigree.
  3. *Side / Angle Profile*: 45-degree angle showing depth, pavilion, and structure.
  4. *Scale / Lifestyle Context*: Natural drape or neckform presentation for proportion.

---

## 3. Chicken Curry Kurta Photography & Model Identity Guidelines
The Kurta collection comprises **50 unique kurta designs** across **5 colorways each** (250 variants), photographed on **50 distinct Indian female model identities**.

### 3.1 Model Identity Integrity
- **One Unique Model per Design**: Each of the 50 designs is assigned a permanent, realistic Indian female model persona (distinct facial structure, skin tone, hair styling, and posture).
- **Cross-Colour Consistency**: Within any single design (e.g., Design 01 "Aria"), the exact same model identity wears all 5 colour variants (e.g., Ivory, Blush Pink, Sage Green, Powder Blue, Lavender). Facial features, height, hair, and body proportions remain visually identical across all 5 colours.
- **Authentic Representation**: Realistic Indian models reflecting diverse natural beauty, elegant poise, and understated ethnic styling without artificial or uncanny AI artefacts.

### 3.2 6 Mandatory Angles Per Variant
Every colour variant features a minimum of 4–6 high-resolution angles:
1. **Front View (`front`)**: Full-length or knee-length straight shot displaying yoke embroidery, neckline, and overall silhouette.
2. **Right-Profile View (`right`)**: 45° to 90° turn highlighting sleeve cut, side slits, and side embroidery motif.
3. **Left-Profile View (`left`)**: Symmetrical alternate view verifying fit and fabric drape.
4. **Back View (`back`)**: Clean back posture highlighting back yoke, tie-ups, neck keyhole, and rear fall.
5. **Close-Up Embroidery Detail (`detail`)**: High-magnification capture of delicate Chikankari stitches (shadow work / *Bakhiya*, *Phanda*, *Keel Kangan*, and *Murri*).
6. **Editorial / Modal View (`modal`)**: Cinematic soft-focus environmental shot for hero sliders and lookbooks.

---

## 4. Bullion Coins Photography Standards (Strictly Isolated)
- **Zero Human Models**: Bullion coins (24K Gold 999 and 999 Fine Silver) must NEVER feature human models, hands, or mannequins in primary artwork.
- **Isolated Numismatic Artwork**:
  - High-precision orthographic top-down and 15° angled relief views.
  - Razor-sharp rim serrations, mirror proof field, frosted frosted relief, and assay purity stamps.
  - Transparent PNG master assets rendered over dynamic luxury coin capsules and velvet presentation cases.
- **Assay Packaging**: Blister pack certifications and tamper-evident assay security cards.

---

## 5. Zero-Watermark & Asset Integrity Enforcement
- **Prohibited Artefacts**:
  - Absolutely zero stock photo watermarks (e.g., Shutterstock, Getty, iStock, Adobe).
  - No AI generator watermarks, logos, initials, or signatures (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion).
  - No promotional banners, sale tags, price stamps, or text overlays burned into the image pixels.
- **Quality Gates**:
  - Automated inspection via script: minimum resolution 1200×1600 px (3:4 aspect ratio) or 1000×1000 px (1:1 square).
  - WebP compression with 85% quality factor, preserving sub-millimeter embroidery thread texture and metal reflections.
  - Uncropped thumbnails generated automatically via sharp pipeline into `*-thumb.webp` preserving true aspect ratio.
