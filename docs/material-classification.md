## 3. Surface Finishes Master Registry (10 Finishes)

1. **High Polish Mirror (`high-polish`)**: Ultra-smooth reflective surface buffed with rouge compound.
2. **Matte Brushed (`matte-brushed`)**: Contemporary linear or circular satin texturing that diffuses light.
3. **Antique Oxidation (`antique-oxidized`)**: Controlled surface darkening that accentuates engraved crevices.
4. **Rhodium Anti-Tarnish (`rhodium-plated`)**: Noble platinum-family electroplating preventing oxidation on 925 silver.
5. **Rose Gold Vermeil (`rose-gold-vermeil`)**: Heavy 2.5-micron 18K rose gold plate over sterling silver core.
6. **24K Yellow Gold Dip (`24k-gold-dip`)**: Flawless pure yellow gold flash plating imparting traditional temple glow.
7. **Satin Sheen (`satin-sheen`)**: Soft-touch velvety sheen balance between glossy and matte.
8. **Hammered Artisan (`hammered-texture`)**: Faceted hand-hammered dimples that catch light dynamically.
9. **Dual Tone (`dual-tone`)**: Harmonious juxtaposition of white rhodium and yellow gold plating on one piece.
10. **Vintage Patina (`vintage-patina`)**: Hand-applied museum-grade heritage finish replicating antique royal heirlooms.

## 4. Collections Master Registry (6 Curations)

1. **The Gold Collection (`gold`)**: Certified 18K and 22K yellow gold fine jewellery designed for cherished celebrations and quiet everyday elegance.
2. **The Silver Collection (`silver`)**: Certified 925 sterling silver jewellery and ornaments crafted with enduring brilliance and fine artisan detail.
3. **Royal Bridal (`royal-bridal`)**: Grand statement ensembles featuring Polki, Kundan, and Temple jewellery created for wedding milestones.
4. **Festive Celebrations (`festive-celebrations`)**: Luminous necklaces, jhumkas, and kurtas radiating joy for Diwali, Eid, and family pujas.
5. **Everyday Quiet Luxury (`everyday-luxury`)**: Lightweight, refined rings, delicate pendants, and minimal pearl cuffs designed for modern daily wear.
6. **Traditional Heirlooms (`traditional-heirlooms`)**: Timeless classical motifs celebrating sacred Indian mythology, nature, and architectural craftsmanship.
