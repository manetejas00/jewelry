# Coin artwork and photo workflow

The six catalogue identities were checked against the existing test3 catalogue before generating artwork. Each image is an illustrative rendering, not a photograph or proof of certification. The storefront labels these images “Illustrative image”. Custom merchant uploads are preserved by migration 007.

- COIN-AU-1G: public/assets/coin-art/lakshmi-gold-1g-v1.png
  Subject prompt: ONE SMALL ROUND YELLOW GOLD COIN, 12 mm diameter, nominal weight 1 gram. Front face has a clearly embossed Lakshmi seated on a lotus, delicate lotus border. Engrave only the small accurate markings “999” and “1 g”. No packaging.

- COIN-AU-5G: public/assets/coin-art/lakshmi-ganesh-gold-5g-v1.png
  Subject prompt: ONE ROUND YELLOW GOLD COIN, 20 mm diameter, nominal weight 5 grams. Front face depicts Lakshmi seated on a lotus beside the recognisable elephant-headed Ganesha, two distinct elegant raised relief figures together on the same coin, subtle mandala border. Engrave only “999” and “5 g”. No packaging.

- COIN-AU-10G: public/assets/coin-art/royal-emblem-gold-10g-v1.png
  Subject prompt: ONE ROUND YELLOW GOLD COIN, 25 mm diameter, nominal weight 10 grams. Front face has an ORIGINAL ornamental royal-style shield crest with restrained acanthus leaves and a symmetric decorative crown, NOT any actual country's or royal family's emblem. Engrave only “999” and “10 g”. No packaging.

- COIN-AG-10G: public/assets/coin-art/floral-silver-10g-v1.png
  Subject prompt: ONE ROUND BRIGHT SILVER COIN, 28 mm diameter, nominal weight 10 grams. Front face has an intricate symmetrical radial floral rosette, finely engraved petals and filigree border. Engrave only “999” and “10 g”. Cool white silver, absolutely no yellow gold. No packaging.

- COIN-AG-50G: public/assets/coin-art/lakshmi-ganesh-silver-50g-v1.png
  Subject prompt: ONE LARGE THICK ROUND BRIGHT SILVER COIN, 45 mm diameter, nominal weight 50 grams. Front face depicts Lakshmi seated on a lotus beside the recognisable elephant-headed Ganesha, two distinct elegant raised relief figures on the same coin. Ornamental lotus border, substantial milled rim. Engrave only “999” and “50 g”. Cool silver, not gold. No packaging.

- COIN-AG-100G: public/assets/coin-art/cast-bar-silver-100g-v1.png
  Subject prompt: ONE RECTANGULAR CAST SILVER BULLION BAR, 55 x 30 mm, nominal weight 100 grams. The bar has softly rounded corners, thick bevelled edges, subtle real cast-metal grain and an elegant recessed central panel. Engrave only “999” and “100 g”. It MUST be rectangular, NOT a round coin. Cool white silver, no yellow gold, no figurative design or crest. No packaging.

Shared prompt direction: Photorealistic 3D catalogue illustration, portrait 4:5. One coin filling about 63% of the frame, front slightly angled, midnight-blue velvet and a warm ivory stone ledge with restrained champagne highlights. Exact metal, motif, shape, 999 purity and gram weight from the subject. No hands, chains, pendant holes, extra coins, fake certification marks, assay cards, third-party logos, serial numbers or watermarks. The 100 g silver item is a rectangular bar. Generated with the built-in image generator; original outputs retained in Codex generated_images. Versioned project PNGs are the production assets.

Photo editing: Admin → Products or Coins → Edit → Choose/Change photo → Save. JPG, PNG, WebP or GIF, up to 8 MB. Drag and drop works too. Browse saved photos lets you reuse uploads and all six named coin illustrations. No URL, file naming convention or image-role selection is required for the main photo. Multiple-photo management remains available under an optional advanced section.

Viewer: open a product image or a coin’s image in its details. Zoom 100–500%, pinch or scroll, drag to inspect, double-tap to zoom, reset and Escape to close. Jewellery galleries support arrows and thumbnails.
